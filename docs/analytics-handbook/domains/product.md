# Product Domain

> **Owner:** Merchandising / Inventory
> **Update Frequency:** Real-time / Daily

## Context: Product Performance

> **Description:** Sales velocity and revenue contribution.
> **dbt Source:** `dim_products`, `order_line_items`

### 1. Units Sold

> **dbt Model:** [fact_sales](../../../transformation/models/marts/sales/fact_sales.sql)

- **Business Definition:** Total quantity of products sold in confirmed orders.
- **Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  SUM(quantity)
  ```
- **Velocity Metric:**
  ```sql
  SUM(quantity) * 1.0 / NULLIF(COUNT(DISTINCT DATE(created_on)), 0) as daily_velocity
  ```

### 2. Product Revenue

> **dbt Model:** [fact_sales](../../../transformation/models/marts/sales/fact_sales.sql)

- **Business Definition:** Gross revenue generated by the product.
- **Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  SUM(line_amount)
  ```

### 3. Profit Margin by Category

> **dbt Model:** [fact_sales](../../../transformation/models/marts/sales/fact_sales.sql)

- **Business Definition:** Gross profit percentage aggregated by category.
- **Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  (SUM(revenue) - SUM(cogs)) / SUM(revenue) * 100
  -- Detailed:
  (SUM(oli.line_amount - oli.quantity * p.cost_price) * 100.0 /
   NULLIF(SUM(oli.line_amount), 0))
  ```

### 4. Return Rate

> **dbt Model:** [fact_sales](../../../transformation/models/marts/sales/fact_sales.sql)

- **Business Definition:** Percentage of units returned.
- **Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  Returns / Units_Sold
  ```

## Context: Inventory Health

> **Description:** Stock levels and efficiency.
> **dbt Source:** `fact_inventory`

### 5. Inventory Turnover

> **dbt Model:** `fact_inventory`

- **Business Definition:** How many times inventory is sold and replaced over a period.
- **Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  COGS / Avg_Inventory
  ```

### 6. Sell-through Rate

> **dbt Model:** `fact_inventory`

- **Business Definition:** Percentage of inventory sold relative to what was available in a period.
- **Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  Units_Sold / (Start_Stock + Received)
  ```

### 7. Days of Supply

> **dbt Model:** `fact_inventory`

- **Business Definition:** Number of days current stock will last based on daily sales rate.
- **Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  Current_Stock / Daily_Sales_Rate
  ```

### 8. Out of Stock (OOS) Rate

> **dbt Model:** `fact_inventory`

- **Business Definition:** Percentage of SKUs currently out of stock.
- **Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  Count(OOS_SKUs) / Total_SKUs
  ```

### 9. Inventory Value

> **dbt Model:** `fact_inventory`

- **Business Definition:** Total capital tied up in stock.
- **Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  SUM(quantity * cost_price)
  ```

### 10. Slow-Moving Stock (Dead Stock)

> **dbt Model:** `fact_inventory`

- **Business Definition:** Products with no sales in the last 90+ days.
- **Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  WHERE days_since_last_sale > 90
  ```
- **Detailed Logic (SQL):**
  ```sql
  SELECT p.product_name, pv.quantity, MAX(o.created_on) as last_sold
  FROM products p
  JOIN product_variants pv USING (product_id)
  LEFT JOIN order_line_items oli USING (variant_id)
  LEFT JOIN orders o USING (order_id)
  GROUP BY 1, 2
  HAVING DATEDIFF(day, MAX(o.created_on), CURRENT_DATE) > 90
      OR MAX(o.created_on) IS NULL
  ```
